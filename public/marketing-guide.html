Marketing studio user guide

The Marketing studio (/marketing-admin) has five pages, shown as tabs. Each chapter below explains one page: what it is for, every section and setting, and a short how-to.

PageUse it to
Logo studioDesign or upload logos, save up to 4, and pick the one used everywhere.
Help videosPlace short how-to videos next to the places in the product where clients need them.
Video finderSearch YouTube for tutorials and ideas.
Landing pages and campaignsBuild promotion pages and compare how each campaign performs.
AI sandboxMake images, videos and music with fal.ai for marketing.

Things that work on every page

  • Sections open and close. Click a section title to open or close it. Expand all and Collapse all are at the top of each page.
  • Help icons ?. Hover or tap the ? next to a section title or a field. It explains the field and links to the matching chapter here.
  • Messages. A green or red line near the top of the page says whether the last action worked.
  • Roles. Staff with look-only roles can see these pages but cannot change anything. Only the owner can delete landing pages and change sandbox limits.

Logo studio

The Logo studio makes the logo for a company and applies it everywhere. In the Marketing studio it edits PaintersMax's own logo. Painters use the same tool at /logo-studio for their company (the dashboard task "Add your logo" opens it).

Your saved logos

Four slots. Each saved logo shows a preview, whether it was designed or uploaded, and three buttons:

ButtonWhat it does
Use this logoMakes it the logo in use. It then appears on documents, emails, the customer portal, city pages, the website header and the browser tab. The previous one stays saved.
EditReopens a designed logo in the editor. Saving replaces it. Uploaded logos cannot be edited; upload a new file instead.
DeleteRemoves it and frees the slot. If it was in use, the logo setting is cleared.

Create my 4 starter logos (free) appears when no logos are saved. It makes one logo for each concept using your colors, so you can compare. Remove all saved logos deletes everything (your free design saves stay used).

Preview before you decide. Save several logos, press Use this logo on one, open your website or a document in another tab, and refresh. Switch to another saved logo in one click. Nothing is lost when you switch.

Credits. For painters, the first 4 design saves are free (the starter set). After that every design save or update costs 1 AI credit, shown on the page. Uploading a logo is always free. PaintersMax's own saves are always free.

Upload your own logo

  1. Open Upload your own logo and choose a PNG, JPG, WebP, GIF or SVG file (up to 8 MB).
  2. The page converts it: a clean PNG, extra empty space trimmed, plus a square icon for the browser tab. Nothing hidden (such as location data) is kept.
  3. If your logo sits on a white background, tick Remove a white background and check the preview.
  4. Name it and press Save uploaded logo. Then press Use this logo on its card.
A transparent PNG at least 600 pixels wide gives the best result. If the page says the file is too large, export a smaller version.

Download files

Downloads use the design on screen, even if it is not saved yet. The name uses a standard bold font; ask a printer to convert text to outlines if needed.

Where your logo is used

  • Documents and emails: estimates, invoices, work orders, flyers, business cards, emails.
  • Customer portal and city pages.
  • Website: every template shows the logo in use in the header and as the browser tab icon. Changing templates keeps the logo. If you chose a different photo as the website logo in the website editor, that photo wins on the website.
  • Dashboard and admin: browser tab icon and a small icon in the top bar. The public paintersmax.app pages use PaintersMax's logo in use.

Changing the logo in use updates all of these. The browser tab icon changes the next time a page loads (browsers cache icons, so a refresh may be needed).

Help videos

Put a short how-to video next to the place in the product where a client will need it. Clients see a "Watch a short help video" button there. A spot with no video shows nothing, so you can add videos one at a time.

Add a video to a spot

  1. Record a 1 to 3 minute video and upload it to YouTube. Choose Unlisted and leave Allow embedding on.
  2. Copy the link (watch link, youtu.be link or Shorts link).
  3. In Help videos, paste it in the row for the spot.
  4. Pick Wide for a normal video or Tall for a phone video or Short.
  5. Type an English and a Spanish title (up to 80 characters each). They become the button text. Leave empty for the default.
  6. Press Save. Press Clear to remove the video from that spot.
ColumnMeaning
Where it showsThe place in the product, with its code name.
YouTube link and shapeThe video and whether it is wide or tall.
TitleThe button words in English and Spanish.
Make one video per job: show the finished result first, then the steps, then the button to press. Make a Spanish version as its own video and use its link in a Spanish-only title later.

Today the button appears on the dashboard home, the AI use and credits page and the top of the website editor. Other spots are listed so you can prepare videos; a developer adds a spot to a page with one line.

Video finder

Search YouTube for tutorials and ideas about painting, business and marketing. It uses the YouTube key stored in Cloudflare. If the page says search is off, the key is missing.

Searching

  1. Type at least 3 letters (up to 80 characters), for example how to paint kitchen cabinets.
  2. Press Search or Enter.
  3. Each result shows a thumbnail, title, channel and a link. Click the thumbnail to watch on YouTube; copy the link into a help video or landing page.
Results are saved for 7 days, so repeating a search costs nothing. New searches count against a free daily YouTube limit; the platform stops at 80 new searches a day and says "resting for today". Only embeddable videos are shown, with strict safe search.

Landing pages and campaigns

Build a promotion page for PaintersMax at paintersmax.app/l/<name> and see how each campaign performs. Pages work in English and Spanish and can show a video.

Tracking links

Pick a page and a source (facebook, instagram, google, youtube, email, text, flyer, truck, partner), optionally add a label, and press Make link. Use that link wherever you share the page. The source shows in the Sources column.

Campaign playbook

A checklist: one goal, one audience, one promise, show the product in a video, tag every link, judge by rates not totals, change one thing at a time, and write an end date in the small print. Rule of thumb: a tap rate under 5% means change the headline; many taps with no sign-ups means change the offer or the sign-up page.

AI sandbox

Make images, videos and music for PaintersMax marketing with fal.ai models. It spends the PaintersMax fal.ai balance, never a client's credits. Nothing you make is public until you press Use in landing page or Make public, and public pages say it was made with AI.

Limits (owner only)

SettingMeaning
Sandbox onMaster switch. Off means nothing can run.
Most per run $A run estimated above this is blocked.
Daily limit $ / Monthly limit $Spending caps (Central time day, calendar month).
Keep items (days)Items you did not star or publish are removed after this many days.

The status line shows whether the fal.ai key is connected, whether the sandbox is on, and today's and this month's spending.

Make something

  1. Choose Image, Video, Image to video or Music or voice.
  2. Optional: press a starter prompt to fill the prompt box.
  3. Pick a model: search by name (up to 60 characters) or choose from the list, or type an exact model id and press Use.
  4. Fill the model's settings (aspect ratio, length and so on) or leave them empty for defaults. Boxes for long text (such as Text) are resizable: drag the corner.
  5. Write the Prompt (up to 2,000 characters; every text box shows a character counter). Keep real people's faces and other brands out. Leave Add safety words on.

Price and run

Press Check price. You see an estimate, how much you have spent today, and any block. If the price is a rough guess, tick the box to confirm. Then press Run. If you change anything after checking, check the price again. Prices are estimates from fal; your fal.ai dashboard shows real spending.

Running

Shows jobs that are in line, being made or being saved. You can keep working. Failed jobs show the reason and are not charged to your limit.

Library

Filter by All, Images, Videos, Sound, Favorites or Public. Each item has: star (keep forever), Download, Copy link (private link works for anyone who has it), Use in landing page (makes it public and shows it on that page with a made-with-AI label), Animate (images only), Make public / Make private, Reuse prompt and Delete (owner).
