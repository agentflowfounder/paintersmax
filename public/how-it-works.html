How it works

Set it up once. Use it on every job.

Here's the whole thing, start to finish, in the order you'll do it.

1. Start free

Make your account with your email. There's no password to remember. We email you a sign-in link, or you can sign in with Google. No card needed.

2. Set up your business (seven short steps)

Your company info, the services you offer, the cities you serve, your prices, your labor and money settings, your warranty and insurance, and a quick review. Everything saves as you go, so you can stop and come back.

For prices, you pick a starting level for each service: Value, Standard, Premium or Master. Then you change any number to match your own costs. The starting prices are ranges to review, not promises.

3. Share your quote page

Your quote page and chat take a customer's details and photos at any hour. The lead lands in your account and your email, so it's waiting for you in the morning.

4. Make the estimate

Open the job, tell it about the work (type it, or tap the mic and talk), add photos, and tap Create estimate. The AI drafts the scope and works out quantities. Your own price list sets the prices. You check it, change anything you want, and preview what your customer will see.

5. Get it signed

Send the estimate or contract by email. Your customer signs on their phone from a private link, and you both get the signed copy.

6. Run the job

Put it on the calendar, assign the crew, and print the materials list and instructions in English or Spanish. If the customer wants extra work, make a change order so the price stays right.

7. Get paid

Invoice by milestone: deposit, progress and final. Turn on friendly reminders if you like. When the work is done, ask the customer to accept it and send a warranty letter.

Nothing goes to your customers until you send it. AI drafts are drafts: read them, fix them, then send.
