Questions we hear a lot

Is there a setup fee?

No. The price on the plan is the price you pay. There is no separate setup fee and no monthly bill. You pay once a year, and the first year includes setting up your website address and your site.

Is Free really free?

Yes. You can use the free tools for as long as you like with no card. You also get 250 welcome AI credits to try the AI tools. They expire after 60 days. After that, you can keep using the free tools or add credits whenever you want.

What happens after the first year?

Local Pro renews at $199 a year, Contractor at $249 and Commercial at $299. You can cancel any time before the renewal date from your dashboard or by emailing billing@paintersmax.app. If a renewal is missed, your website, your directory listing and the free tools stay on. Only the paid extras stop.

Can I get my money back?

Yes. If you cancel within 14 days of your first payment, we refund it in full, less $20 for the website address we registered for you.

What are AI credits?

Credits pay for the heavier AI jobs, like reading a photo, making a color preview or helping with a quote. Every account gets 250 welcome credits. Paid sign-ups get 500 more (1,000 during Booked by Spring). If you want more, you can add a monthly allowance ($19 for 150 credits, $39 for 400, $79 for 1,000, and unused credits roll over for two months) or a one-time top-up from $5 to $100. The credits that expire soonest are used first, and if an AI try fails, the credits come back.

Will you contact my customers?

No. PaintersMax only emails your customers when you choose to send something. AI drafts never go out until you send them.

Who owns the website address?

We register it for you and keep it renewed while your plan is active. If you ever leave, you can ask to transfer it to you until it expires.

Does it work in Spanish?

Yes. The app and the crew documents work in English and Spanish. Our chat helper, Maistro, answers in Spanish too.
