Set up the business

    Build business credit

    1. Open a business bank account in the business name, and run every job through it.
    2. Use the same business name, address and phone everywhere: state filing, bank, suppliers, Google profile.
    3. Open trade accounts with paint stores and rental yards, and pay on time or early. Ask if they report to business credit bureaus.
    4. Keep invoices, receipts and job results in PaintersMax. Clean records help when a lender or a builder asks.

    Financing, for you and your customers

    • Customer financing: lets a homeowner pay over time for a big project. Compare the fee you pay per job.
    • Equipment leasing: spreads the cost of sprayers, lifts or a trailer before a big commercial or government job.
    • Line of credit: covers payroll and materials while a big customer takes 30 to 60 days to pay.
    • SBA-backed loans: longer terms for growing businesses. See sba.gov loans.

    Read every agreement and compare at least two offers. PaintersMax does not lend money or recommend a lender.
