How to bring a design from Canva
  1. Design it in Canva. Use the size you need (business card, letter page, door hanger).
  2. Tap Share, then Download. Pick PNG or JPG for a picture, or PDF Print for a print shop.
  3. Come back here, choose the file, pick what it is, and tap Upload.

Soon you will be able to order printed business cards, shirts and door hangers from our print partners straight from here.
