Market price levels

Starting rates are multiplied by these numbers for each client's market. 1.00 is the average market. These are my starting guesses, not market data: change them as you learn what your clients see. Allowed range 0.50 to 2.00.

Materials list

The list painters see at /materials and everyone sees at paintersmax.app/painting-materials. Add a photo address (https) and a how-to for each item. Add buy links one per line as: Label | https://link | affiliate (write the word affiliate only if it is an affiliate link). The affiliate disclosure appears only when at least one link is marked affiliate. Photo uploads will come after R2 is on.

Help guide and questions

Painters ask questions on the Help page. When the guide cannot answer one, the question (never the painter's name or company) lands here. Draft an answer with AI if you want, edit it, then approve it. Nothing joins the guide until you save it. Editing a starter article saves your own copy, which replaces the starter.

Client domains

For a client with their own domain: add the domain to your Cloudflare account, add it to the paintersmax-site worker (Workers, Settings, Domains and Routes, Add custom domain), then connect it here. Add both the bare domain and the www version if you want both. The client still signs in at paintersmax.app.
