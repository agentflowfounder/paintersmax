Vendor badges

Small animated badges for paint stores and suppliers. Use them in posts, pages, help articles and settings. They are our own designs: the store's name in our font next to a painting icon, in the PaintersMax colors. They are not the stores' logos.

All badges

Each badge plays once when it appears. Point at one, or tab to it with the keyboard, to play it again.

How to use them

1. The stylesheet. Every PaintersMax app page already has it. On any other page, add this line inside <head>:

<link rel="stylesheet" href="https://paintersmax.app/pm-badges.css">

2. The badge. Write the store's name inside a span with two classes: pmv and the store's class.

<span class="pmv pmv-home-depot">Home Depot</span>

3. Options. Add more classes in the same quotes, in any order.

ClassWhat it does
pmv-sherwin-williamsPaint can, the lid pops and settles
pmv-home-depotPrice tag swings on its string
pmv-lowesTape measure rolls in and the blade slides out
pmv-benjamin-mooreColor swatch cards fan open
pmv-ppgPaint roller rolls on a fresh stripe
pmv-otherBrush paints a stroke. Use for any other store or supplier, with its name inside.
pmv-light, pmv-goldLight or gold look, for light backgrounds
pmv-sm, pmv-lgSmaller or larger
pmv-tileSquare icon only, 44 by 44, easy to tap on a phone
pmv-loopRepeats the motion. One per screen at most.
pmv-hostPut on a card or link around a badge so hovering the whole card plays it again

Rules that keep us safe

  • No store logos. Company logos, colors used as branding, and slogans are trademarks. We use a real logo only after that company gives us written permission. Until then, these badges are the way to show a store.
  • Name the store accurately. Write its real name, spelled the way the store spells it. Do not suggest the store sponsors or endorses us or the painter unless it has agreed in writing.
  • Keep the PaintersMax colors. Do not recolor a badge to look like a store's own branding.
  • Calm motion. Badges move only with transform and fade, stop moving for people whose device asks for reduced motion, and still show fully if scripts are off.
