Privacy Policy

PaintersMax (paintersmax.app). Last updated November 10, 2026.

What we collect

  • Account information: your name, business name and email address.
  • Sign-in with Google: if you choose it, we receive your Google email address and whether it is verified. We use it only to find your PaintersMax account. We do not read your Google mail, contacts, files or calendar.
  • Business and job data you enter: estimates, contracts, invoices, material lists, and customer names, emails, phone numbers and job addresses.
  • Basic usage data: sign-in sessions and feature usage counts, so we can run the service and enforce plan limits.

How we use it

To run your account, create and send the documents you ask for, sign you in, bill you, and keep the service secure. We do not sell your data or your customers' data.

Who else handles it

  • Stripe processes payments. We never see or store full card numbers.
  • Cloudflare hosts the service and stores the data.
  • Resend delivers the emails we send for you.
  • AI providers receive job details (not customer email, phone or street address) when you use the AI assistant or similar features, only to produce your answer. If you upload an insurance policy, the file is sent to an AI provider to be read. We keep only the summary and the text it read, not the original file, and you can delete them at any time from the Insurance page.

Your choices

You can ask us to export or delete your account and data at any time by emailing us. Your customers' information in your jobs belongs to your business.

Security

Sign-in links are single use and expire. Sessions are stored securely and end after 14 days. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data.
