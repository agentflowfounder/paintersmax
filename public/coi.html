This page is a summary prepared by the contractor from their own policy. It is not an official certificate of insurance and does not change the policy. If you need an official certificate, ask the contractor to have their insurance agent send one. Secure page by PaintersMax.

Before the job starts
  • You can check that this policy is active by calling the carrier named above. Have the contractor's company name ready.
  • Ask the contractor for an official certificate of insurance if you, your landlord or your building manager require one.
If something happens during the job
  1. If anyone is hurt, call 911 first.
  2. Tell the contractor right away so they can report it to their insurance company.
  3. Take photos or video of the damage before anything is moved or repaired.
  4. Keep receipts, messages and estimates for any repair.
  5. Do not sign anything or accept a payment you are unsure about until you understand it. You can ask the contractor for their insurance contact.

General information only. The insurance company decides what is covered.
