Features

Everything a job needs, in the order a job happens.

No giant manual. Here's what's inside, grouped by what you're trying to get done.

Start freeSee pricing

Get found and get leads

  • A quote page and contact form that work on any phone
  • Instant quote and AI chat on your website (Launch and up)
  • A listing in the painter directory
  • Customer reviews, only from customers who paid

Win the job

  • Estimates with what's included and not included
  • Your own rate card: pick Value, Standard, Premium or Master, then change any number
  • Photo help for tricky jobs
  • Color previews on your customer's own photo

Sign and schedule

  • Contracts your customer signs on their phone
  • Site visits, calendar and crew assignments
  • Work orders and change orders for extra work
  • Materials and tools lists

Get paid

  • Invoices by milestone: deposit, progress, final
  • Friendly payment reminders you can turn on
  • Customer sign-off and a warranty letter
  • A morning email with what needs you today

Stay organized and covered

  • Job records in one place
  • Insurance helper and certificates of insurance
  • Crew time sheets and records for your accountant
  • Reminders for expiring insurance and open items

Built for your whole team

  • English and Spanish for you, your crew and your customers
  • A help chat that answers from the guide
  • A materials list page painters can share
  • Clear AI limits so nobody runs up your bill
