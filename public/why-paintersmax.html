Why PaintersMax

Built by a painter. Not a software company.

Most contractor software was made by people who never had to chase a deposit, explain a change order or find out the crew showed up without the right color.

PaintersMax is built around how a job really goes: the phone call, the walkthrough, the estimate, the signature, the crew, the extra closet the customer adds on day two, and the final check. It's made to be fast on a phone, in English and in Spanish, because that's how crews really work.

What we promise

  • We don't make things up. No invented reviews, no fake numbers, no pretend before-and-after photos. AI pictures are always labeled previews, and a preview isn't a paint match.
  • We never contact your customers on our own. They hear from you, when you choose to send something.
  • Your data is yours. You can download your records any time your account is open.
  • AI that can't run away with your bill. Every AI tool has limits per visit, per day and per plan, and we can switch any tool off at once. See it in action on the Color Studio.
  • Honest about the legal stuff. Records are for your accountant, not tax advice. Contracts are templates for you and your attorney to shape. Insurance notes are general information.

Who's behind it

PaintersMax is a product of AgentFlow LLC. Questions, ideas or something broken? Write to support@paintersmax.app. A person reads it.
