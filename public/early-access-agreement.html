PaintersMax Early Access Agreement

Version 2, November 15, 2026. This agreement has not yet been reviewed by an attorney. It is written for a short early-access period and will be replaced by reviewed Terms of Service. It lowers risk for both of us; it does not remove it.

1. Who this is between

This agreement is between AgentFlow LLC, which runs PaintersMax ("PaintersMax", "we"), and the painting company that signs up ("you"). It adds to the PaintersMax Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. If this agreement and the Terms say different things, this agreement wins during early access.

2. What early access means

PaintersMax is new software and it is still changing. During early access:

  • Features may be added, changed or removed.
  • You may find mistakes. Please tell us at support@paintersmax.app so we can fix them.
  • We may pause the service for short periods to fix or improve it.

Early access ends when we tell you by email that PaintersMax is out of early access, or when either of us ends this agreement.

3. What PaintersMax is, and what it is not

PaintersMax is a tool that helps you run your painting business: a website and quote page, leads, estimates, contracts, invoices, a calendar, crew time records, job records and reminders.

PaintersMax is not a lawyer, an accountant, an insurance agent or a licensed contractor, and it does not give legal, tax, accounting or insurance advice. In particular:

  • Estimates, contracts and invoices are yours. The templates are a starting point. You are responsible for the prices, the wording, and for following the laws and license rules where you work. Have your own attorney look at the contract you use.
  • AI can be wrong. Some features use AI to suggest prices, read photos, receipts and documents, answer questions and write messages. Read and correct everything before you send it or rely on it. Nothing AI writes is sent to your customer until you choose to send it.
  • Insurance notes are general information. "Appears to cover" is a plain comparison with the summary you saved. It is not a coverage decision. Only your insurance company or agent can tell you what is covered.
  • Records are for your accountant. Reports, exports, mileage, time sheets, overtime notes and 1099 lists are records to hand to your accountant. They are not tax or payroll advice.
  • Color previews are previews. They are not a color match. Test a sample before you buy paint.

4. Your customers and their information

You decide which customers you put into PaintersMax and which emails you send them from it. You are responsible for having their permission to contact them. PaintersMax sends an email to your customer only when you choose to send it or turn that notice on.

We use your customers' information only to run the service for you. We do not sell it. See the Privacy Policy for details.

5. Your data

Your business data is yours. You can download your records at any time while your account is open. If you close your account, you may download your data for 30 days afterward. After that we may delete it.

6. Price, billing and refunds

  • A free account costs nothing.
  • A paid plan (Local Pro, Contractor or Commercial) is a yearly fee, paid at sign-up through Stripe. Your price is the one shown when you signed up. Each year after that renews at the renewal price shown at sign-up, unless you cancel before the renewal date.
  • Monthly AI credit allowances and one-time credit top-ups are optional. They are charged only when you choose them, and you can stop an allowance at any time from your dashboard.
  • You can cancel at any time from your dashboard or by emailing billing@paintersmax.app. Canceling stops future charges.
  • Refund: if you cancel within 14 days of your first payment, we refund it in full, less $20 for the website address (domain) we registered for you.
  • If a renewal is not paid, your website, your listing and the free tools stay on; only the paid extras stop.
  • AI features have monthly amounts that depend on your plan. When a monthly amount runs out, that feature waits until the next month unless you have AI credits. Credits given free (welcome, sign-up and promotion credits) can be used until the date shown on your Credits page. Credits you buy as top-ups do not expire during early access.

7. Fair use

Do not use PaintersMax to break the law, send spam, upload other people's private information without permission, or try to break or copy the service. We may pause an account that does.

8. The service is provided "as is"

We work hard to make PaintersMax reliable, but during early access we provide it as it is, without promises that it will always be available or free of mistakes.

9. Limit on what we owe each other

To the extent the law allows, PaintersMax's total responsibility to you for any claim about the service is limited to the amount you paid us in the 12 months before the claim. Neither of us is responsible to the other for lost profits or indirect losses.

10. Changes and ending

We will email you at least 14 days before any change to this agreement that matters. Either of us can end this agreement at any time. Sections 3, 5, 8 and 9 continue after it ends.

11. Law and contact

This agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Texas. Questions go to support@paintersmax.app. Legal notices go to legal@paintersmax.app and to AgentFlow LLC, 409 Lindsey Ln, Tyler, TX 75701.

How it is accepted: a checkbox at sign-up. We save the date and time, your email and the version of this agreement that you accepted.
